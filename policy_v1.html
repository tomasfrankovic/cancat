What This Policy Covers

This policy explains what data is collected when you play SYNTAXIA (the demo or any future release), how it is used, and what choices you have. SYNTAXIA may be available on multiple platforms including Steam, itch.io, CrazyGames, and other WebGL-compatible platforms.

This is policy version 1 (policy_v1), accessible via a link in the game and on our store pages. If this policy is ever updated in a meaningful way, a new versioned policy will be published and you will be asked for fresh consent in-game before any change takes effect.

Data We Collect

// Analytics and Crash Reporting (Unity)

If you give your consent at first launch, we collect anonymous gameplay and technical data through Unity Analytics and Unity Cloud Diagnostics (crash reporting). This may include:

  • Session length and frequency
  • In-game events and actions (e.g. levels started, completed, failed)
  • Device type, operating system, and game version
  • Crash logs and error reports

This data is processed by Unity Technologies. You can read their privacy policy at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

We do not collect analytics data unless you explicitly accept at first launch. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy.

// Community Statistics (PlayFab)

SYNTAXIA shows community statistics — for example, what percentage of players made the same choice as you at key moments (similar to Detroit: Become Human or The Walking Dead). These statistics are anonymous aggregate counters. Your individual choice increments a counter; no personal data or player identifier is attached to it.

To support this feature, a randomly generated player ID is created locally on your device (for example: itch_a3f9b2 or steam_x7k2m1). This ID is not linked to your real identity, your Steam account, or any other personal information. It is stored in Microsoft PlayFab solely to prevent duplicate contributions to statistics.

You can disable community statistics and delete your PlayFab profile at any time in Settings > Privacy. Deleting your profile removes the random ID from our servers. Because statistics are anonymous counters, deletion does not subtract from previously recorded totals.

PlayFab is operated by Microsoft. Their privacy information is available at playfab.com/terms.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name, email address, or any contact details
  • Your Steam ID or any platform account identifier
  • Payment or financial information
  • Location data
  • Any data from children under 13 — we do not knowingly collect it

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

CanCat Games is based in Slovakia, an EU member state. We process data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Analytics and crash data — processed on the basis of your explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting any previous processing.

Community statistics — because no personal data is attached to these counters, GDPR does not apply to them. We mention them here for full transparency.

Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Withdraw analytics consent at any time (Settings > Privacy)
  • Request deletion of your PlayFab profile (Settings > Privacy > Delete my data)
  • Ask us what data we hold about you
  • Lodge a complaint with the Slovak data protection authority (UOOU) at uoou.sk

To exercise any right, contact us at cancat.games@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Data Retention

Analytics and crash/error data is linked to an anonymous installation ID generated automatically by Unity, along with basic device information such as OS and device type. This data is automatically deleted after 90 days.

If you wish to request earlier deletion, contact us at cancat.games@gmail.com and we will submit a removal request to Unity on your behalf.

Your PlayFab random ID and associated data are retained until you request deletion via Settings > Privacy, or contact us directly.
